Camera

See what the drone's camera would make of you. A small person detector runs in this tab. The page follows only the person holding the printed marker, blurs everything else, and the repo's follow logic says what the drone would do.

Point it only at people who agree. Nothing is recorded or uploaded: the frames stay in this tab and are dropped once drawn. Everything outside the marked person's box is blurred before it is drawn, and nobody else is tracked.

Print the marker

Print it at least 15 cm across, on any printer, and hold it flat in front of your chest. The detector finds people; the marker tells it which one is the rider. Without the marker, nobody is followed.

How it works

  • People: YOLOX-Nano finds people in each frame, in this tab, about 70 to 120 ms a frame on a laptop.
  • The rider: the person whose box holds the marker. If the marker is hidden, the same person is kept for one second by box overlap, then let go.
  • Privacy: the whole picture is blurred, then only the rider's box is drawn sharp. A missed or unknown person stays blurred.
  • The follow logic: the rider's box goes to the repo's own Python, run here by Pyodide: the camera estimate, the safety margin, the mode and the command, as in the replay viewer. Hide the marker and watch the modes fall back.
  • Distance comes from the box's height, assuming a 1.75 m person and the field of view above. It is rough.
  • Range: in 500 COCO photos the detector found about 85% of people closer than 5 m, about two thirds at 5 to 11 m, half at 11 to 16 m, and few beyond 22 m, with a 70° camera.

Cost explorer

What the repo's cost model says the drone costs. Each pass is one way of getting the parts, from buying everything at retail to designing boards and having a factory assemble them.

Estimates, not quotes. Prices were checked up to the cutoff date, and many are older or are guesses, as the price table below shows. Labour, tooling, failed builds, crashes, rework, storage and shipping are not included. Nothing has been ordered.

Cost per drone as the number of drones grows

For exploring only: the owner has not set a number of drones. Each point is the cash to build that many, with every order at least its minimum, divided by the count.

It assumes one order per design for the whole batch and the same unit prices at every count, so real volume discounts would lower it. The PCB assembly setup and loading fees are paid once per order; the import-duty allowance stays per drone.

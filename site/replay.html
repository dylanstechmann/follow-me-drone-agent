    Replay your own log

    A file you open here stays in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded. Make a trace from a log with the replay tool, then open it above:

    PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m follow_me.replay --json LOG.json > trace.json

    The log format is described at the top of src/follow_me/replay.py. Download the example: the log or its trace.

    How to read it

    • Attention scales a source's weight by conditions: rain lowers the camera, a weak link or fix lowers a companion, and a known trouble spot can switch a source off.
    • Trust is the reading's reliability after attention. Zero means the source is off.
    • The gate rejects a reading that sits more than about three sigma from the gate centre, a reliability-weighted median of the active readings.
    • The fused estimate combines the kept readings, weighting each by trust divided by variance.
    • Modes count ticks, not seconds. The example has two ticks per second, so return-home is requested about 50 s after the last estimate.

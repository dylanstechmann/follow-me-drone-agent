Scenario sandbox

Run a pennyboard follow shot, retrieve a soccer ball on land or shallow water, pick up a soda across a living room, relocate a pennyboard, or configure a generic object. Scrub the mission, inspect each event, and switch between an external orbit and the simulated onboard view.

This is a simplified synthetic kinematic simulation. Nothing has flown. No drone, waterproof tentacle, gripper, or other hardware exists or has been tested. This is not PX4 or ArduPilot SITL, not realistic fluid or contact physics, and not evidence that a mission is safe or possible. The onboard view is simulated footage, never a recording.

06 / Model boundary

What this does not prove

  • Fixed-step positions and speeds are scripted kinematics. There is no aerodynamic model, battery, wind, collision, obstacle avoidance, autopilot, or flight validation.
  • The tentacle is one idealized prismatic line with a fixed gripper offset. There is no physical tentacle, gripper design, waterproofing test, contact dynamics, or payload demonstration.
  • Water is a translucent visual plane with fixed-depth illustrative buoyancy and zero drag, not fluid physics. Non-waterproof water missions stop before submerging; the page never changes the checkbox to make them pass.
  • Object dimensions and masses are illustrative, not measured product or hardware specifications. Generic mass only changes the payload check.
  • The follow rider is synthetic and explicitly consenting. There are no bystanders, real people, real footage, coordinates, or routes.
  • The onboard camera is ideal and stabilized, with no lens distortion, occlusion, detection noise, or recording. Framing statistics describe this model only.
